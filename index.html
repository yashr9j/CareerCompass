Welcome to CareerCompass

CareerCompass is a free, student-focused career guidance portal built for engineering undergraduates — especially those pursuing Computer Science, AI & ML, and related disciplines. Whether you are in your first year wondering where to begin, or in your final year preparing for placements, this portal gives you structured, actionable guidance at every step.

From curated career roadmaps and internship strategies to resume templates and skill checklists, CareerCompass is your one-stop navigation system for building a successful tech career.

Why Start Career Planning Early?

The Reality of Campus Placements

According to the All India Survey on Higher Education (AISHE) 2023, only about 40–45% of engineering graduates secure jobs directly through campus placements. The rest rely on off-campus applications, internship conversions, and competitive exams. Early, structured career planning dramatically improves your odds.

What Recruiters Actually Look For

  • Strong fundamentals in Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA)
  • At least one domain specialization (Web Dev, ML, Cybersecurity, etc.)
  • Practical project experience with GitHub portfolio
  • Internship or research experience (even 1–2 months matters)
  • Communication skills and problem-solving aptitude
  • Competitive programming ratings (LeetCode, Codeforces, CodeChef)

Year-wise Career Planning Overview

Recommended Career Activities by Academic Year (B.Tech CSE / AI-ML)
Year Focus Area Key Activities Target Outcome
1st Year Foundations Learn Python, C; join coding clubs; solve basic DSA; set up GitHub Strong programming base, active GitHub profile
2nd Year Skill Building Pick a domain (Web/ML/App); build 2–3 projects; start competitive programming Domain clarity, first mini-projects, LeetCode 50+
3rd Year Internships & Specialization Apply for summer internships; contribute to open source; attend hackathons Internship certificate, 3–5 solid projects, LeetCode 150+
4th Year Placements & Higher Studies Prepare for aptitude + DSA interviews; apply off-campus; GATE/GRE if needed Job offer / admit letter, strong resume, interview confidence

Popular Career Tracks for CSE / AI-ML Students

  1. Software Development Engineer (SDE) — Backend, Frontend, or Full Stack at product companies
  2. Machine Learning Engineer / Data Scientist — Building and deploying ML models at scale
  3. Data Analyst — Extracting business insights from structured data using SQL, Python, and BI tools
  4. DevOps / Cloud Engineer — Infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, AWS/GCP/Azure
  5. Cybersecurity Analyst — Ethical hacking, network security, SOC operations
  6. Research & Academia — M.Tech / MS / PhD path via GATE, GRE, or direct research programs
  7. Product Manager (Tech) — Product strategy, roadmap, and cross-functional collaboration
