Frequently Asked Questions

Is career counseling on this site free?

Yes, completely free. CareerCompass is a student-built project developed as part of the Web Technologies Lab at S-VYASA Deemed University. All guidance is provided voluntarily by the team.

I have a very low CGPA. Can I still get a good tech job?

Yes. Many product companies (especially startups) do not have a CGPA cutoff. A strong GitHub portfolio, competitive programming rating, and good DSA skills can easily compensate for a lower CGPA. Service companies like TCS and Infosys do use CGPA filters (typically 6.0–7.5), but product roles are skill-first.

Should I do an M.Tech or go for a job directly?

It depends on your goals. If you are targeting research, academia, or specialized roles (quantitative finance, core AI research), M.Tech or MS abroad adds significant value. If you want to work in product development, startups, or industry ML, a direct job with strong self-learning often gives you a head start — you can always pursue higher studies later via part-time or sponsored programs.

How many projects should I have on my resume?

Quality beats quantity. 2–3 well-documented, deployed projects with clear problem statements, tech stacks, and impact metrics are far more impressive than 10 half-finished hobby projects. Every project on your resume should have a live demo or GitHub link you can speak confidently about in an interview.
